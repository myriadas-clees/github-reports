# Myriad Brand Guidelines

Source: `branding/Myriad_2024-2025_Brand_Guidelines.pdf`, `branding/Myriad_2025_Letterhead_Final2.0.docx`, and `branding/Myriad_2025_Letterhead_Final_Print.pdf`.

## Identity

- Brand: Myriad Advisor Solutions.
- Short form: Myriad.
- Do not use Myriadas, MyriadAS, Myriad AS, or similar concatenations in copy. The website domain `myriadas.com` and email `info@myriadas.com` are contact details, not brand names.
- Mission: Empower businesses to focus on achieving their goals by alleviating operational demands and challenges through all-encompassing solutions, guidance, and support.
- Vision: Be the premier solution for entrepreneurial and business operations.
- Tagline: Do what you do BEST! Let us do the REST! (registered)
- Voice and tone: consistent, authentic, professional, supportive, and accountable to the client experience.

## Logo

- Primary logo: full Myriad Advisor Solutions lockup.
- Submark: compact M mark for small spaces or constrained placements.
- Use the primary logo on white or light backgrounds.
- Do not use the primary full-color logo on dark backgrounds.
- Use the solid black logo when the logo must appear in one color.
- Use the reverse logo on dark backgrounds only when the red mark has enough contrast against the image or color.
- Preserve clear space around the logo. Clear-space distance X equals the width of the stem on the M.

## Typography

- Primary font: Roboto.
- Roboto weights shown in the guidelines: Thin, Light, Light Italic, Regular, Italic, Medium, Medium Italic, Bold, Bold Italic, Black.
- Secondary font: Lora.
- Lora weights shown in the guidelines: Regular, Italic, Bold, Bold Italic.
- Use Roboto for operational UI, labels, navigation, headings, and dense information.
- Use Lora sparingly for editorial accent text, refined section titles, pull quotes, or body copy where the brand should feel warmer.

## Graphic Elements

- Approved icons may be used in any brand color.
- Keep icons simple, functional, and consistent with the approved icon sheet.
- Favor red, black, cool gray, or white icon usage depending on contrast.

Reference asset: `assets/reference-pages/approved-icons.png`.

## Wallpaper and Backgrounds

- Use `assets/official-assets/wallpaper-myriad-v05.jpg` when a dark branded wallpaper or presentation/video-call background is requested.
- Treat the wallpaper as a branded background asset, not as the default style for every page.
- Keep foreground content legible on the dark textured field; use white text, sparing Myriad red accents, and generous contrast.
- For normal documents and product UI, prefer the white letterhead/product pattern unless the user explicitly requests a dark branded background.

## Imagery

Use:

- Simple, professional, diverse imagery.
- Internal imagery when possible.
- Clear business operations, advisory, team, and client-support contexts.

Avoid:

- Overly posed photos.
- Hands as the center focus.
- Common/free clip art.
- AI-generated or AI-looking art.

## Editorial Style

- Refer to the company as Myriad or Myriad Advisor Solutions only; never Myriadas, MyriadAS, or Myriad AS.
- Follow AP Style unless an exception below applies.
- Use Merriam-Webster for spelling and definitions.
- Spell out abbreviations on first use, such as pay-per-click (PPC).
- Abbreviate street types and compass directions in addresses, such as St., Ave., Blvd., NW, and SE.
- Use advisor, not adviser.
- Use the Oxford comma.
- Write out month names; do not use ordinal dates such as 1st or 2nd.
- Write email as one word.
- Use headline capitalization with a mix of capital and lowercase letters. Keep headlines concise, aiming for 45 characters or fewer.
- Spell out numbers under 10; use numerals for 10 and above.
- Spell out percent in text; use `%` in headlines.
- Italicize book, movie, and similar titles; use title case.
- Use web lowercase and website as one word.
- Use a single space after periods and colons.
- Use AM and PM uppercase with no periods.
- Use double quotation marks and avoid scare quotes.
- Refer to companies as it.
- Respect proper noun spellings such as 3M and Big Ten.
- Use hyphens for compound adjectives, such as well-known author and high-quality product.
- Do not hyphenate adverbs ending in -ly before adjectives.
- Format phone numbers as `123-456-7890`.
- Use `www` only when necessary.
